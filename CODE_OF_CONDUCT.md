# Code of Conduct

PrepUp is a small project, and everyone who takes part in it (issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews) is expected to help keep it a place people want to be.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are learning, which is the point of the project.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Say what would make it better.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Keep it relevant to PrepUp.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about who someone is.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately derailing a conversation.

## If something goes wrong

Contact the maintainer, [Solivagant14](https://github.com/Solivagant14), through the details on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue. Reports are kept private.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block someone from the project when this code is broken.

This applies in every PrepUp space on GitHub.
